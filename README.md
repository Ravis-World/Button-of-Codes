# 🦃 BUTTON OF CODES: Thanksgiving Horror

> *"It’s 3:14 AM on Thanksgiving Night in Ward 4B. Cold leftover turkey sits wrapped in foil on your desk. Out in the dimly lit hallway, an ancient entity known as **The Carver** looms—feeding on tryptophan drowsiness, fear, and human error..."*

**Button of Codes: Thanksgiving Horror** is a fast-paced, text-based terminal horror game built in pure Lua. Operate the hospital's emergency console and resolve **19 escalating emergency protocols** in sequential order before your alertness hits zero. One wrong command, and Ward 4B belongs to the Carver.

## 🕹️ Key Features

* 📟 **Pure Terminal Immersion:** Retro-style, text-driven CLI gameplay reminiscent of classic emergency dispatch terminals and early mainframe games.

* 🩸 **19-Level Gauntlet:** Face 19 sequential emergency codes ranging from de-escalation protocols to active fire, hazmat, and bomb threats.

* 🧠 **Puzzles & Multi-Step Defusal:** Test your real-world triage knowledge, calculate pediatric dosages, and decipher cryptograms under high-stakes pressure.

* 💀 **Permadeath Loop:** Lose your alertness, and experience a system failure as the entity consumes the console.

* ⚡ **Zero External Dependencies:** Built with pure Lua 5.1+—no dynamic dynamic C libraries required. Runs natively across Windows, Mac, and Linux.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Launch the game** in your system terminal.

2. Read each emergency protocol alert carefully.

3. Input the required **command**, **calculation**, **triage tag**, or **puzzle cipher**.

4. Clear all 19 ranks sequentially to survive until the morning shift!
